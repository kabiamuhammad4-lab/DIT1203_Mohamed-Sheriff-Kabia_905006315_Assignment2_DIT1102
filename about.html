About Us

Learn who we are and why we do what we do.

Started by farmers, for farmers

GreenHarvest began when a group of young people noticed that good crops were going to waste in the villages while buyers in the city could not find them. We decided to build a simple link between the two.

Today we work with farmers and buyers across the country. We help with the price, the delivery and the storage, so that both sides can trust the deal.
