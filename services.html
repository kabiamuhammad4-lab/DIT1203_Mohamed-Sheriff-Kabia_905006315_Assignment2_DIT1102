How it works

Four easy steps.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Send us a message or call. Say the crop, the amount and where you are.

  2. We find the right match

    We look for a farmer or a buyer that fits your request.

  3. Agree on price and details

    We help both sides agree on a fair price, the date and the delivery.

  4. Receive or deliver the crops

    The crops move, the buyer checks them, and the farmer gets paid.

Frequently asked questions

Who can use GreenHarvest?

Any farmer who has a harvest to sell and any buyer who needs crops, from a single family to a restaurant or shop.

How do I sell my crops?

Send us a message from the Contact page with the crop, the amount and your location. Our team will call you and list your crop.

How is the price decided?

We look at the current market price and agree on a fair price with the farmer before we show the crop to buyers.

Do you deliver?

Yes. We can help with transport and storage. The cost depends on the distance and the amount of crop.

Need help with a service?

Our team is ready to answer your questions.
